Method

  • One manual search per trade on Oct 15, 2026; first 20 non-sponsored results recorded by hand. Only the star rating, review count and listing link were recorded. No review text, photos or map images.
  • Boundary: City of Hamilton (including Stoney Creek, Dundas, Ancaster, Glanbrook, Flamborough). Listings with an address or map pin outside the city were dropped. Addressless (service-area) listings were kept unless their pin or name pointed elsewhere.
  • Dropped: listings whose Google category did not match the trade, and listings with zero reviews.
  • Duplicates: two listings for the same brand count as one business, using the larger listing.
  • Medians and the under-10 share are calculated on the listings kept for each trade.
  • Only the top 3 per trade by review count are named. Businesses below the median are never named or listed.
  • Most addresses come from the map pin and were not opened one by one. Leaders were opened and checked where marked confirmed in our notes.
  • Exclusions on this pull:
    • Electrical: 1 listing, category is Corporate office
    • Electrical: 1 listing, map pin outside Hamilton
    • Electrical: 1 listing, zero reviews
    • Electrical: 1 listing, zero reviews
    • Electrical: 1 listing, address in Burlington
    • Heating and cooling (HVAC): 1 listing, category is Heating equipment supplier
    • Heating and cooling (HVAC): 1 listing, addressless; name points to another market, Hamilton service not verified
